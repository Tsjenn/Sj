# The Silicon Ledger — getting honest reviews without breaking Amazon's rules

Written 19 September 2026. Amazon removes reviews it thinks were
arranged, and it can remove the book. So the rules first.

## Amazon's rules that apply (Community Guidelines, checked Sep 2026)

- A reviewer must have **spent at least US$50 on Amazon.com in the past
  12 months** with a card. A friend in Malaysia or Singapore with a
  Kindle who has bought books from Amazon.com qualifies; someone who
  has never bought there cannot post, however willing.
- **Family, close friends, employees, and anyone with a financial
  interest may not review.** Amazon links accounts by address, payment
  method and household. Do not ask your family. Do not ask colleagues
  who share your office address.
- **No incentive of any kind** — no payment, no gift, no discount code,
  no "I'll review yours". A free reading copy given before publication
  in exchange for an *honest* review is the long-standing exception,
  and the request must never be conditional on a positive review.
- **Review swaps with other authors are prohibited** and Amazon detects
  them.
- Asking readers for a review is allowed and normal. Asking for a
  *good* review is not.

## Who to ask (in order of likelihood to post)

1. People who have **already read it** and told you so. Send Message 1.
2. Readers on **LinkedIn who commented** on the launch post or on the
   subject: offer a reading copy, ask for an honest review when they
   finish. Send Message 2.
3. **Goodreads** — anyone can review there regardless of Amazon spend,
   and Amazon shows Goodreads ratings on Kindle pages. Malaysian and
   Singaporean readers who cannot meet the US$50 rule can still help
   here. Same messages, different link.
4. The **finance and semiconductor people you already know
   professionally** who are not colleagues at your employer. A reading
   copy and Message 2. Twenty of these produce more reviews than any
   ad.

## Message 1 — to someone who has read it

    Hi [NAME],

    You mentioned you'd finished The Silicon Ledger — thank you for
    reading something that long.

    One small ask. Amazon shows new books to more readers once a few
    reviews exist, and right now it has almost none. If you have two
    minutes, an honest review — whatever you actually thought, including
    what did not work — would help more than anything else I could do.

    The form is here: https://www.amazon.com/review/create-review?asin=B0HG7RHNL4
    (Amazon only lets people review if they've bought something from
    Amazon.com in the past year; if that isn't you, Goodreads works too.)

    Either way, thank you.

    Shiuan Jenn

## Message 2 — offering a reading copy

    Hi [NAME],

    I've just published a book that follows the money through the AI and
    semiconductor industry — the filings, not the press releases: TSMC,
    ASML, Nvidia, memory, the clouds, the model labs and who owns whom.
    Seventy-seven chapters, every figure dated and sourced, no price
    targets.

    Given what you work on I think you'd have opinions about it, and I
    would rather have them than not. May I send you a reading copy? No
    obligation at all — but if you do read it, an honest review on
    Amazon or Goodreads when you're done would help it find readers.

    Shiuan Jenn

Attach nothing until they say yes. Then send the EPUB
(dist/The-Silicon-Ledger.epub) or the PDF, with the line: "Honest
review, positive or not — that's the only kind that helps."

## Message 3 — inside the book itself

Add a short page at the very end of the ebook (after the last chapter,
before the appendices) — Amazon allows this:

    If this book was useful, a review on Amazon helps the next reader
    find it. Say what you actually thought; that is the kind that counts.
    [link]

This is the highest-yield review request there is, because it reaches
every reader at the moment they finish. It needs one rebuild of the
EPUB and a re-upload; tell me and I will add it.

## What to expect

Of twenty people asked, a handful will say yes, and one to three will
post within a month. That is normal. Three reviews is the threshold at
which the ads test (AMAZON-ADS-PLAN.md) becomes worth running.

## Never

Buy reviews, use a review service, ask family, ask colleagues at your
employer, offer anything for a review, or ask for five stars. Any of
these can remove every review the book has and the book with it.
