THE HONEST MECHANICS FIRST

Amazon shows books that already sell. A brand-new book with no reviews
gets almost no organic visibility no matter how good its keywords are.
That is the cold-start problem and it is real.

So there are exactly three ways to get seen:

  1. PAY for it (Amazon Ads). Reliable. Costs money.
  2. BRING your own traffic (LinkedIn, your site). Free. Costs time.
  3. CONVERT better when someone does land. Free. This is A+ Content.

There is no fourth way, and nothing here is automatic. I cannot run
your ads, post to your accounts, or get you reviews — those need your
account, your money and your name. What I have done is make each of
them a copy-paste job instead of an afternoon.

Do them in this order: 3, then 2, then 1. Fix the page before you send
anyone to it, or you pay to send people to a page that does not sell.

================================================================
STEP 2 — BRING YOUR OWN TRAFFIC (FREE, 10 MIN A DAY)
================================================================

Not TikTok. LinkedIn. The buyer for this book is a finance
professional, and on LinkedIn "chartered accountant who read all the
filings" is credible in a way it can never be on TikTok.

Ten posts are written for you in LINKEDIN-POSTS.txt. One a day. Do not
sell the book in the post — post the useful fact, and let the book sit
in your profile. People who find you useful will go looking.

Rules that matter on LinkedIn:
  - No external link in the post itself. It suppresses reach. Put the
    Amazon link in the FIRST COMMENT, and in your profile's Featured
    section.
  - First two lines are everything — the rest is hidden behind
    "see more".
  - Reply to every comment within the first hour.

STEP 1 LAST — AMAZON ADS (COSTS MONEY, ONLY AFTER A+ IS LIVE)
================================================================

Do not start until the A+ content is approved and live.

CAMPAIGN SETUP
  Type:            Sponsored Products
  Targeting:       Manual
  Bid strategy:    Dynamic bids — down only
  Daily budget:    Start at USD 5. Not more. This is a test, not a

  Default bid:     USD 0.30
  Run for:         14 days before you judge anything

PRODUCT TARGETS — target the detail pages of books your reader already
buys. Search each on Amazon, copy its ASIN, paste into Product
Targeting:

  Chip War (Chris Miller)
  The Everything Store
  Too Big to Fail
  The Big Short
  Barbarians at the Gate

This is the highest-intent targeting available to you: people already
looking at exactly this kind of book.

WHAT TO WATCH AFTER 14 DAYS
  Impressions under 1,000  -> your bid is too low, raise to 0.45
  Clicks but no sales      -> the PAGE is the problem, not the ad
  ACOS above 100%          -> normal at first for a new book; it means
                              you are paying more than you earn per
                              sale. Decide if that is worth it for the
                              ranking, or stop.

BUDGET DISCIPLINE
  Set a hard monthly cap you would not mind losing entirely. Treat the
  first month as tuition, not investment. If USD 150 over a month
  produces no sales, stop and put the money nowhere — do not double it.


================================================================
WHAT I WILL NOT SUGGEST, AND WHY
================================================================

Buying reviews, review-swap groups, or asking friends to post verified
reviews they did not earn. All of it violates Amazon's terms, gets
books delisted, and it is the one thing that would genuinely destroy a
chartered accountant's credibility if it surfaced.

The book's whole positioning is that it does not fake numbers. Faking
its reviews would be the exact same lie, one level up.


================================================================
THE HONEST EXPECTATION
================================================================

A+ content will lift conversion. LinkedIn will bring dozens, not
thousands. Ads will cost more than they return at first, and may never
break even on a single title.

None of that adds up to a living. It adds up to a book that sells
occasionally and a name that becomes known in a small professional
circle — which is worth something, but as a credential, not an income.

The income is in the plan in INCOME-PLAN.txt, and it is not on Amazon.
