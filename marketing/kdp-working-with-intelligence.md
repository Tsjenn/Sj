## Description (paste into the description box)

Everyone is using AI. Almost nobody is using it well.

Most professionals have typed a question into an AI assistant, received something
impressive, and quietly wondered two things: how much of this can I trust, and am I
being left behind? This book answers both — without hype, and without the invented
statistics that fill most books on the subject.

Written by a chartered accountant for working professionals, it teaches the skill
itself, from the ground up:

- HOW THE MACHINE ACTUALLY THINKS — a five-minute mental model that predicts, in
  advance, where AI will impress you and where it will quietly embarrass you.
- THE CRAFT OF PROMPTING — the six-part frame behind every good prompt, twelve
  failure patterns with their fixes, and a library of forty reusable professional
  prompts.
- BEYOND THE CHAT BOX — projects, your own documents, agents, tools, and AI that
  works directly in your files; what each is for, and when each is overkill.
- MAKING IT RELIABLE — grounding, evaluation, and the Three-Check Rule: a
  verification habit light enough that busy people actually keep it.
- SIXTEEN INDUSTRY PLAYBOOKS — finance, audit, tax, banking, insurance,
  manufacturing, supply chain, retail, healthcare administration, legal, property,
  education, marketing, HR, customer service, and the solo professional. Each with a
  value map, ten concrete use cases, worked prompts, the sector's real risks, and a
  30-day starting plan.
- IMPLEMENTATION — a 90-day adoption plan, a training curriculum, honest measurement,
  and a sample AI policy you can adapt.
- RISK AND CAREER — bias, ownership, regulation, AI-enabled fraud, when NOT to use
  AI at all, and what a professional career looks like from here.

With a diagram in every chapter and tables throughout.

No miracle claims. No invented research. No promises about your income. Just an
honest, thorough, practical guide to becoming the person in your organisation who
uses this technology well — and knows exactly where to stop.

## Honesty notes for this title (do not remove)

- The book contains no invented statistics, studies, surveys, adoption rates, or ROI
  figures, and no fabricated case studies. Keep it that way in all marketing copy.
- Do not claim endorsement, partnership, or certification by any AI vendor.
- Do not claim the book guarantees income, savings, or job security.
- Chapter openings are second-person scenes ("It is half past four and...") or generic
  professional situations. They claim no specific experience for the author and name no
  real person, client, or firm. This was a deliberate policy decision partway through
  drafting; earlier first-person anecdotes were removed.
- Every legal, regulatory, tax, clinical and professional passage carries a stated
  disclaimer that it is general information, that rules differ by jurisdiction and
  change, and that the reader must check their own position.
- Chapter 61 (regulation) deliberately names no statute, regulator or case anywhere, so
  the book stays true across jurisdictions and does not date.
