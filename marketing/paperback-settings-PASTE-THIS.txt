AI WITHOUT THE HYPE - PAPERBACK SETUP ON KDP

WHY THE UPLOAD FAILED BEFORE
You were in the Paperback flow but uploading the ebook files.
Paperback will not accept an EPUB interior, and it will not accept a JPG
cover. It wants a PDF interior and a single PDF wrap cover. Those two
files are now attached. Nothing was wrong with your account.

DO THE EBOOK FIRST (it is already ready)
Bookshelf > Create > Kindle eBook
Manuscript: AI-Without-The-Hype.epub
Cover:      AI-Without-The-Hype-cover.jpg
Publish. Then come back and do the paperback.

THEN THE PAPERBACK
On the finished ebook, click "Create Paperback". That copies the title,
subtitle, author, description, keywords and categories across, so you do
not retype anything.

SETTINGS TO CHOOSE - these must match the files exactly

Print options
  Ink and paper       Black & white interior with white paper
  Trim size           7 x 10 in  (choose "Select a different size")
  Bleed settings      Bleed (PDF only)
  Cover finish        Matte   (glossy also fine - your taste; matte suits
                      a dark navy cover and hides fingerprints)

Manuscript
  Upload              AI-Without-The-Hype-INTERIOR.pdf
  Page count          795 (KDP reads this itself; do not type it anywhere)

Cover
  Choose              "Upload a cover you already have (print-ready PDF)"
  Upload              AI-Without-The-Hype-WRAP-cover.pdf
  Do NOT open Cover Creator - it will overwrite this file.

ISBN
  Take the free KDP-assigned ISBN unless you already own one.

AI content disclosure
  YES. Say AI was used for text and images, and that you reviewed and
  edited the work. Tick it on the paperback too, not just the ebook.

WHAT THE FILES ALREADY HANDLE FOR YOU
  Trim              7 x 10 in
  Interior margins  0.875 in left and right (KDP requires 0.875 in gutter
                    for books of 601-828 pages), 0.75 in top, 0.85 in bottom
  Page numbers      centred in the footer
  Diagrams          all 68 converted to greyscale for black ink printing
  Spine width       1.79 in, calculated from 795 pages on white paper
  Bleed             0.125 in on all four outside edges
  Cover size        16.04 x 10.25 in at 300 dpi
  Barcode area      left clear on the lower right of the back cover, which
                    is where KDP prints its own barcode

TWO THINGS TO EXPECT
1. KDP may report the page count as 796. It pads odd page counts by one
   blank leaf. That is normal and does not require a new file.
2. The limit for black ink on white paper is 828 pages. At 795 you are
   inside it with room to spare. If you ever add material and cross 828,
   the fix is cream paper or splitting the book into two volumes.

BEFORE YOU HIT PUBLISH
Open the KDP Print Previewer and check four things:
  - the title page
  - one part divider
  - one diagram (any chapter opening)
  - Chapter 18 or Chapter 35, which are the table-heavy stress tests
If the previewer flags anything, tell me the exact wording and I will
regenerate the file.

PRICE
Your decision. KDP will show you the minimum list price it will allow,
because a 795-page paperback has a high printing cost. It will be higher
than the ebook by a wide margin. Set it wherever you want above that
floor.
