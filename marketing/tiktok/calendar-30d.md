**Option A — my clips (zero editing):** I generate ready MP4s in
`dist/clips/` (real gameplay, real soundtrack, caption burned in).
Download → open TikTok → upload → paste caption → post.
Ask me for fresh clips any time: they're one command for me.

**Option B — your recording (more personal, often performs better):**
Screen-record yourself playing (Control Center → record), play 30-60s,
trim the ends in Photos or CapCut, post with the caption below.

never use anyone else's audio/characters. Our music is our own.
