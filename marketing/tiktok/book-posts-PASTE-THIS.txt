TIKTOK POSTS FOR THE THREE BOOKS

================================================================
READ THIS FIRST — TWO THINGS YOU NEED TO KNOW
================================================================

1. I CANNOT POST TO TIKTOK FOR YOU.
   There is no TikTok connection in this session. I can make the videos
   and write the posts; you upload them from your phone. It takes about
   two minutes each.

2. NONE OF THESE THREE BOOKS IS ON AMAZON YET.
   I checked. The only two live Amazon links in your store are the two
   toddler books. The AI book, MATCHA and the novel are all built and
   waiting on your upload, so there is no Kindle link to put anywhere.

   That is why each video comes in two versions:

       book-<name>-coming.mp4   says "Coming to Kindle"
       book-<name>-live.mp4     says "On Kindle now"

   Post the "coming" one today if you want. Post the "live" one the day
   the book goes up. Do not post the "live" one before the book exists.

3. ABOUT THE LINK. TikTok captions do not make links clickable. A URL
   pasted into a caption is just grey text nobody can tap. The link goes
   in your BIO, and the caption says "link in bio". Set your bio link to
   your Amazon author page once you have one, so all three books sit
   behind a single link you never have to change.

================================================================
HOW TO POST — one video, about two minutes
================================================================

1. Save the .mp4 to your iPad Photos (tap the file, then Share > Save
   Video).
2. Open TikTok, tap +, tap Upload, pick the video.
3. Skip all the editing screens — the video is finished.
4. Paste the caption, then the hashtags after it.
5. Cover image: TikTok will ask you to pick a frame. Choose a frame from
   the FIRST three seconds — that is the hook line. Do not pick the book
   cover; the cover is the payoff, not the opener.
6. Post.

Do not post all three on the same day. One a day, three days running,
is better — TikTok spreads your reach across them instead of making
them compete.


================================================================
WHAT IS AND IS NOT CLAIMED IN THESE VIDEOS
================================================================

Every line in the videos is checkable against the actual books:
chapter counts, prompt counts, playbook counts, recipe count, and the
plot of the novel. There are no sales figures, no rankings, no review
quotes, and the word "bestseller" appears nowhere.

The music is original and generated for these clips, so nothing here
can be muted for a copyright claim.
