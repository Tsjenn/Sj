ABOUT THE AMAZON LINK — READ THIS, IT SAVES YOU A MISTAKE
================================================================

TikTok captions do not make links clickable. A URL pasted into a caption
is grey text nobody can tap. It also looks like spam to the algorithm.

The link goes in your BIO. The caption says "link in bio".

Best setup: put your AMAZON AUTHOR PAGE in the bio, not one book's link.
Then all three books sit behind one link, and you never change the bio
again when you publish something new. You get an author page free at
author.amazon.com once any book of yours is live.

Send me the three Kindle links (or your author page link) and I will:
  - put them into site/config.js so your own store links them
  - add them to the guides that mention each book
  - keep them in one place so nothing goes stale

I do not have those links — only you can see them in your KDP account.

HOW TO POST — about two minutes each
================================================================

1. Save the .mp4 to your iPad Photos (tap the file, then Share > Save
   Video).
2. Open TikTok, tap +, tap Upload, pick the video.
3. Skip the editing screens — the video is finished.
4. Paste the caption, then the hashtags after it.
5. Cover frame: TikTok asks you to pick one. Choose a frame from the
   FIRST three seconds — that is the hook line. Do NOT pick the book
   cover; the cover is the payoff, not the opener.
6. Post.

Do not post all three on the same day. One a day, three days running.
TikTok spreads your reach across them instead of making them compete.

Reply to every comment in the first hour. On a small account that is
the single biggest thing you control.


================================================================
WHAT IS AND IS NOT CLAIMED
================================================================

Every line in the videos is checkable against the actual books: chapter
counts, prompt counts, playbook counts, recipe count, and the plot of
the novel. No sales figures, no rankings, no review quotes, and the word
"bestseller" appears nowhere. Keep it that way — a new book with an
invented ranking is the fastest way to lose the one thing you are
selling, which is that you do not make things up.

The music is original and generated for these clips, so nothing can be
muted for a copyright claim.
