# Seedance 2.5 prompt formula for 15-second product ads

Seedance follows a shot description far better than a mood description.
Write the prompt like a camera operator's notes, not like a poem.

## The formula (one shot, 5 seconds, 9:16)

    [Subject with the reference image] · [action in the shot] · [camera
    move] · [lighting] · [setting details] · [texture words] · vertical
    9:16, 5 seconds, photoreal, shallow depth of field, no text, no
    logos, no people's faces

Example, bakery:

    A butter croissant from the reference image, being torn open by two
    hands, steam and flaky layers visible · slow push-in from a low
    angle · warm morning window light from the left · marble counter,
    a linen cloth, a white coffee cup slightly out of focus · glossy
    lamination, crumbs falling · vertical 9:16, 5 seconds, photoreal,
    shallow depth of field, no text, no logos, no faces

## The three-shot structure

1. **Hook (0–5 s):** the product at its most physical — steam, pour,
   crumb, fabric, water, stretch. Motion in the first second.
2. **Use (5–10 s):** the product in the customer's hands or space — a
   table, a sofa, a mat, a shelf. Still no faces; hands and backs only.
3. **Reveal (10–15 s):** the whole product or the shop front feel, the
   camera pulling back, calm. This is where the end card lands.

## What to upload as references in Omni mode

- One clean product photo from their public Instagram (their actual
  croissant, chair, reformer, serum bottle). Mark it as the subject.
- Optionally one photo of their interior for shot 3.
- No logos. No faces. Faces are where AI video still fails and where
  the business will feel something is off.

## Negative instructions that matter

"no text, no logos, no faces, no watermarks, no extra fingers, no
melting, steady geometry, consistent product between shots".

## Settings

Seedance 2.5 in Dreamina: ratio 9:16, 1080p if available (720p is
fine for Instagram), duration 5 s per shot, generate two, keep one.
Turn generated audio off — music is added in the finishing step so
every ad has the same clean sound and no stray voices.
