# First ten spec-ad targets (found 19 September 2026, from public listings)

All ten are real businesses with a public Instagram or website found in
food and lifestyle guides this year. Contact goes through their
Instagram DM or the contact form on their site — never through a
scraped email. Confirm each is still open before you generate; cafés
move. Internal file: names of businesses are fine here; the owner's
own city and employer stay out of every message sent.

## Per-business concept, prompt and message

### 1. The Lightwood by Mues
**Concept:** "The crust does the talking." Hook: a sourdough loaf cut,
crumb opening. Use: focaccia torn over a wooden board. Reveal: pastel
counter, morning light.
**Shot 1 prompt:** A rustic sourdough loaf from the reference image being
sliced by a serrated knife, open crumb and crackling crust · slow
push-in, low angle · soft morning window light · pale wood board, pastel
pink wall out of focus · flour dust, crust shards · vertical 9:16, 5
seconds, photoreal, shallow depth of field, no text, no logos, no faces
**Caption line:** Baked before you woke up.
**End card:** The Lightwood by Mues · [their hours] · [their handle]
**DM:** "Hi — I made a 15-second video of your sourdough because I think
it deserves one. It's attached, it's yours, no strings. If you'd like
three a month for your Instagram, I do that. Either way, enjoy it."

## The DM, in general

    Hi — I made a 15-second video of your [product] because I think it
    deserves one. It's attached; it's yours, no strings. If you'd like
    three a month for your Instagram, I do that. Either way, enjoy it.

Three lines. No pitch, no price, no story about you. The ad is the
pitch.
