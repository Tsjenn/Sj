DESCRIPTION — paste this whole block

Your team is already using AI. There is probably no written rule about it.

This is a working pack, not a book. Nothing in it needs reading cover to
cover. It is a set of artefacts you can put in front of a team on Monday.

WHAT IS IN IT

40 prompts that work, grouped by workflow — month-end close and
reconciliations, variance analysis and commentary, budgeting and
forecasting, audit support, tax and compliance prep, contracts and policy
documents, board papers, and process documentation. Each one is pasteable
as written, and each has a line underneath telling you the specific thing
most likely to be wrong in its output.

Three policy documents. A one-page team note for a team that needs a rule
by Friday. A full firm policy with drafting options for a practice that
has clients and a regulator. And the paragraph you put in an engagement
letter when a client asks how you use these tools.

A 90-day adoption plan. Week by week, in three phases: who does what,
what "done" looks like, and the way each phase usually fails.

A four-level training curriculum. Session outlines, learning outcomes and
a live exercise for each level, written so a manager can teach it without
hiring anyone.

A method for choosing what to automate first. Five factors, thirty
finance tasks already scored against them, and a spreadsheet for scoring
your own. Most teams pick the most exciting use case rather than the one
that would actually pay; this is how you tell the difference.

Ten places not to use AI at all, and a vendor checklist to work through
before a tool touches client data.

WHAT IS DELIBERATELY NOT IN IT

No statistics about AI adoption. No productivity percentages. No case
studies about firms that saved a fortune. No promises about your income.

All of that is standard in this market and almost none of it is
checkable. The moment a pack like this quotes a figure nobody can verify,
every other claim in it becomes a guess too. What that costs you: it
cannot tell you what results to expect. What it buys you: everything it
does tell you, you can check.

WHO IT IS FOR

Finance managers and controllers, partners in small accounting and audit
firms, tax practitioners, and heads of finance. No technical background
assumed. It does assume you are busy, sceptical, and personally on the
hook when work goes out wrong.

FORMAT

84-page PDF, plus every document again as plain text you can paste into
your own templates and edit, plus the scoring sheet as a CSV that opens
in Excel, Numbers or Sheets. No analytics, no tracking, nothing phones
home.

Written by Tang Shiuan Jenn, chartered accountant.

Everything touching law, tax, professional standards or regulatory duty
is general information, not legal advice. Rules differ by country and
profession and they change. Have the policy read by whoever carries risk
in your organisation before you adopt it.
