## 1. Show HN (news.ycombinator.com/submit)

**Title:**
Show HN: Build your own sleep score and watch one night score 45 or 95

**URL:** https://tsjenn.github.io/Sj/score-lab/

**First comment (post immediately after submitting):**
I got tired of my sleep app grading my mornings, so I built a page
that turns the tables: one fixed (synthetic, labeled) night of sleep,
five sliders for the weights, and you make the score. The
"re-measure" button perturbs only the deep-sleep estimate, because
that's the input consumer trackers genuinely can't measure well —
under a stage-heavy recipe the same night swings 45–95.

Nothing to install, no signup, no backend, vanilla JS. The preset
recipes are invented for the demo — real ones are proprietary, which
is rather the point.

Honest disclosure: I sell a sleep tracker (it's linked at the bottom).
Its pitch is that it labels what's measured vs estimated, which is why
I wanted this page to exist either way.

**HN norms to respect:** no title-case bait, reply to every comment,
don't argue with criticism — thank and fix.
