**Name:** Rested

**URL:** https://tsjenn.github.io/Sj/sleep/

**Platforms:** Online / Web (works in mobile and desktop browsers)

**License / price:** Free (paid offline version available)

**Description:**
Rested is a browser-based sleep tracker that stores all data locally
on the user's device — nothing is uploaded and no account is
required. It records bedtimes, wake times and sleep-diary notes, and
distinguishes clearly between what it measures and what it only
estimates. The free tier runs entirely in the browser; a paid
downloadable version works fully offline.

**Suggested "alternative to":** Sleep Cycle, SleepScore, Pillow,
Sleep as Android

**Tags:** sleep-tracker, privacy, local-first, web-app, no-signup

Note for the owner: everything above is checkable against the live
app. If moderation asks anything, answer plainly — the local-only
claim is the entire pitch and it's true.
