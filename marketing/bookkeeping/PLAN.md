# The first cents — a plan built on the one skill that buyers already pay for

Written 19 September 2026. You are a qualified accountant who does
bookkeeping. That is the most reliably paid remote service on the
internet, and it is the one thing this repo has never tried to sell.
Everything below is either done today or is a step you can do on the
iPad in under thirty minutes.

## First, the job

Being bullied at work is not something you caused, and nothing in this
plan asks you to put up with it. Two facts and one rule:

- Since 11 July 2025, harassment that causes distress, fear or alarm is
  a criminal offence in Malaysia under Penal Code sections 507B–507G.
  Employers must handle complaints under the Employment Act, and
  retaliation for a good-faith complaint is grounds for an unfair
  dismissal claim.
- Write down each incident — date, what was said or done, who was
  present — in a note on your phone, the same day. A dated record is
  what turns "they are horrible to me" into something HR, the Labour
  Department or a lawyer can act on.
- Do not resign to escape it. The plan below exists so that the job
  becomes optional. When a second income covers your essentials for
  three months, the bully loses the only power they have over you. Until
  then, the salary is the runway and the bullying is a note on your phone.

## What the money looks like (checked September 2026)

- Small businesses pay roughly US$200–600 a month for outsourced
  bookkeeping, and the freelancer tier of that market is filled from
  Upwork and Fiverr.
- Upwork's own figures: median bookkeeper rate around US$15 an hour,
  typical range US$11–25, QuickBooks-certified freelancers US$35–75.
- Three monthly clients at US$300 each is US$900 a month, roughly
  RM3,800, for perhaps 30 hours of work. That is not a fortune. It is
  the runway.

## The gate, and why it comes first

MIA's rules say a member who provides public practice services for a
fee as a sole proprietor, other than as an employee, is "in public
practice" and must hold a practising certificate. Bookkeeping for a fee
is, on the plain reading, inside that. The MIA email in your Gmail
Drafts now asks exactly this question and, if the answer is yes, asks
for the route to a practising certificate. **Send it today.** Until the
reply comes, do the preparation below so that the day the answer
arrives you are listing, not starting. If a practising certificate is
required, apply for it: you are qualified, and the certificate is the
thing that lets you charge for the skill you already have.

## Two tracks, so something earns while the gate is open

### Track A — not gated, start this week

Work that is not public practice and that buyers already pay for:

1. **Upwork, Excel and finance work.** Financial models, cash-flow
   forecasts, data clean-up, dashboards, budget templates. Your profile
   copy and three proposal templates are in UPWORK.md. Apply to five
   jobs a day; a proposal is a hundred words.
2. **The three Fiverr gigs** built this week (web tool, 3D video, KDP
   book), plus **Gig 4, "Excel financial model and dashboard"**, in
   GIG-4-EXCEL.md. Portfolio piece: dist/Monthly-Close-Pack.xlsx, built
   today from sample data.
3. **Certifications that cost nothing and change how buyers read you:**
   QuickBooks Online ProAdvisor (free, about eight hours, badge shows on
   Upwork) and Xero Advisor (free, about six hours). Both are done in a
   browser. Start ProAdvisor this weekend.

### Track B — bookkeeping retainers, the moment MIA says go

1. Upwork profile switches its headline to bookkeeping (the copy is
   ready in UPWORK.md, marked "after MIA").
2. The Fiverr gig becomes "Monthly bookkeeping in QuickBooks Online or
   Xero" with Basic / Standard / Premium at 50 / 150 / 300 transactions
   a month.
3. The first three clients are found on Upwork, not Fiverr — Upwork is
   where monthly retainers are hired. Propose a fixed monthly fee, never
   hourly, and always offer a "first month at the same price, cancel
   any time" to remove the buyer's risk.
4. Deliverable every month: the close pack — P&L, balance sheet, bank
   reconciliation, AR and AP ageing, and a five-line note in plain
   words on what changed. That pack is what keeps a client for years.
