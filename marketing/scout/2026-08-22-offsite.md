# Scout report — off-site venues, verified live (2026-08-22)

Second research pass of the day: where this business can appear beyond
its own site, checked against current rules pages. Tags: [platform-doc]
= official rules, [anecdote] = community/postmortem.

## Verified findings

1. **Show HN** — best single fit for Score Lab ("try it" demos are the
   whole point of the section). Rules: URL in URL field, plain title,
   honest first comment, personal account, never solicit upvotes, one
   realistic shot. [platform-doc]
   https://news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html

2. **r/InternetIsBeautiful** — Score Lab qualifies (interactive, no
   signup) BUT a fresh Reddit account posting its own site gets
   removed; the account needs real history first (90/10 rule). Action:
   owner starts using Reddit normally now, posts in a few weeks.
   [anecdote]

3. **Newgrounds** — free, open HTML5 uploads (index.html at zip root —
   our packaging scripts already produce exactly this), community
   judgment vote, organic front-page promotion not tied to payment.
   Must own all assets/music — we do. [platform-doc + anecdote]
   https://www.newgrounds.com

4. **itch jams** — the real discovery engine on itch, but most jams
   require games made DURING the jam; existing games can't be entered.
   Keep the profile evergreen; only enter if we build in-window (would
   need the human's explicit ask — new game = new product rule).
   https://itch.io/jams/upcoming/with-participants

5. **AlternativeTo** — developers may submit their own app; human
   moderation kills ad copy, neutral descriptions survive. Listing
   Rested as an alternative to Sleep Cycle / SleepScore / Pillow puts
   it exactly where people search. Slow-drip, permanent. [platform-doc]
   https://alternativeto.net/faq

6. **awesome-privacy (GitHub)** — PR per CONTRIBUTING.md (data files,
   one addition per PR, respond to review within 48h). Rested's
   local-only claim is defensible. Agent can prepare or open the PR —
   owner should approve before anything is submitted under their name.
   https://github.com/Lissy93/awesome-privacy

7. **方格子 vocus** — open registration, automatic ad revenue-share
   once eligible, Taiwan-oriented payouts (owner must verify Malaysia
   withdrawal before counting income; distribution value stands
   regardless). Best zh home for 《環境比意志力強》 essays.
   [platform-doc] https://vocus.cc

8. **Dcard 書籍板** — own-work posts allowed, store links banned;
   value-post with the book title only. Owner re-reads pinned 板規
   first. [anecdote]

9. **DEV.to / Hashnode canonical republishing** — both confirmed
   current [platform-doc]. DEV wants the BUILD story ("how I made a
   client-side sleep score toy"), not sleep tips. Referral traffic
   modest; treat as backlink + surface, not a firehose.

10. **Dead ends verified:** Uneed free queue closed 2026-08-17;
    Medium's import tool broken since ~Oct 2025 (manual canonical
    still works); Product Hunt for audience-less indies = a few
    hundred visits then flatline. TinyLaunch/MicroLaunch are the free
    substitutes. [platform-doc + anecdote]

Full source list lives in the research transcript; key URLs above.

## What this changes in launch-kit.md

- r/InternetIsBeautiful moves from first to later (account aging).
- Show HN becomes the lead move for Score Lab.
- Newgrounds becomes the lead move for games (before Reddit).
- vocus becomes the lead move for the zh book.
- Product Hunt and Uneed removed from the plan.
