Look at what you keep sending me: screenshots of people describing how
they run businesses with AI agents. That genre is exploding in
Mandarin right now — and you are not a spectator of that story. You
OWN one: a real fleet, real books shipping daily, a real store, run
by an accountant in Malaysia from an iPad. Nobody has to exaggerate
anything — the true version is already the interesting version.
