# Scout report — deconstructing the top earners (2026-08-22)

Fifth research pass: what the world's top products in our niches
actually do, with the non-copyable parts named honestly. Tags:
[data] documented figures, [anecdote] single accounts, [guru]
flagged and not relied on.

## The one pattern across every documented zero-to-income case

Never "marketing". Always one of three mechanisms:
(a) a platform's native search fed by MANY narrow, well-titled
listings (Rachel Jimenez: CNBC-documented $77-160k/yr from Etsy
printables, zero audience, zero ads);
(b) one intrinsically shareable artifact (Wordle/Sprunki-class —
a free lottery ticket you print by making outputs shareable; note
Sprunki's creator captured almost none of the value);
(c) owned email built by years of free publishing (James Clear:
6 years of essays → 450k list → launch. The list is not copyable;
the cadence is — and it's literally our daily-article model).

## Extracted mechanics already shipped or queued

- Personalization-quiz onboarding (Calm's pattern) → SHIPPED into
  Rested today (three-answer Tonight's plan).
- Morning report + consistency streak (Sleep Cycle + Pokémon
  Sleep's schedule-reward — the mechanic behind a ~$40M/yr app) →
  Rested has streaks; morning-report card upgrade queued.
- Shareable result cards (emoji-grid à la Wordle) → queue for games
  + Rested; zero backend, documented breakout precedent.
- Daily bonus / level map / comeback reward + "first win under 60
  seconds" (Poki's documented first-3-minutes rule) → games queue.
- Named, ownable rules + chapter-end summaries + free cheat-sheet
  funnel (Atomic Habits' structure; the 30M-copy launch machine is
  NOT copyable, the structure is) → books queue. zh book metadata
  should honestly ride 《原子習慣》 search vocabulary (Taiwan's #1
  bestseller 4 years running) — comparison framing, no false
  association.
- Long-tail listing catalog + pricing ladder + in-use mockups
  (the documented Etsy mechanism) → Etsy pack already built to
  this spec; planner variant-splitting queued (prices = owner).
- One outcome-promise headline per product page (Headspace's
  "Meditation made simple" discipline) → site copy pass queued.
