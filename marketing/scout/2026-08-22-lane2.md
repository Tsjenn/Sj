# Scout report — Lane 2 first clients, Malaysia (2026-08-22)

Live research on how a solo lands the first 1-3 SME bookkeeping
clients while employed. Condensed; verify anything marked ⚠ before
repeating to a client. Tags: [official] statute/regulator,
[industry] firm/vendor pages, [anecdote] practitioners.

## The legal lines (matter most)

1. **Restricted titles.** Accountants Act 1967 s.22: only
   MIA-registered persons may use "accountant / chartered accountant /
   auditor / tax consultant / tax adviser" when holding out to the
   public — fine up to RM10,000. Bookkeeping AS A FUNCTION is not
   restricted; the words are. All Lane 2 materials rewritten to
   "bookkeeper / bookkeeping / monthly close". [official]
   https://www.commonlii.org/my/legis/consol_act/aa19671972216/

2. **Tax is a harder line.** Only s.153-approved tax agents may file
   or represent before LHDN. Prepare numbers; hand off. Befriending
   one licensed tax agent = referral channel both ways. [official]
   https://www.hasil.gov.my/en/tax-agent/profession-as-a-tax-agent/

3. **Moonlighting.** Legal in MY; the employment contract governs —
   read for exclusivity / conflict / outside-work-approval clauses
   FIRST. Never use employer time, laptop, or templates. SSM
   registration (RM30-60/yr) once recurring; declare on Form B.
   [industry/official]

## The demand wedge

- **E-invoicing**: Phase 4 (RM1-5M turnover) mandatory since
  2026-01-01; enforcement relaxed ⚠ (industry reports say to end
  2027 — verify on hasil.gov.my). Honest pitch: "clean books and
  e-invoice process before enforcement", never fine-scaremongering.
- **SST expansion (2025-07-01)** pushed more SMEs over registration
  thresholds — they need books to even know.
- **MADANI digital grant**: 50% up to RM5,000 toward accounting
  software; the CLIENT claims it via an MDEC partner (e.g. Bukku) —
  removes the software-cost objection. [official/vendor]

## Pricing (verified market norms)

RM300-500/mo entry (sole prop, <50 txns); RM450-800/mo small Sdn
Bhd. Working solo comp: izzservices.com, from RM300/mo, 16+ clients,
niched into F&B, bilingual pain-topic blog. Fixed monthly packages
dominate. Discounted PAID first cleanup month beats free (free
attracts non-payers). [industry/anecdote]

## Channels (verified open)

- Warm network WhatsApp (the evidence-strongest first-client channel
  in every practitioner account) — referral ask, not sales pitch.
- Malaysian SME Facebook groups — "looking for bookkeeper" posts
  verified to exist; answer helpfully, never advertise. [anecdote]
- **Bukku partner program** — free Malaysian advisor directory built
  for bookkeepers. **QuickBooks ProAdvisor** — free cert + listing.
  Skip Xero directory (needs 8+ Xero clients). [vendor]
