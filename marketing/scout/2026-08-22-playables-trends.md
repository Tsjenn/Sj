# Scout: what wins on YouTube Playables & web portals (2026-08-22)

Purpose: owner asked for more games for the Playables/Playgama
channel, built by copying the SELLING POINTS (mechanics, hooks) of
top performers — never their IP.

## What's actually at the top

- Playables' own top-5 (per coverage): Magic Tiles 3, Race Master 3D,
  Stealth Master, Thief Puzzle, Tall Master. Catalog staples: Angry
  Birds Showdown, Cut the Rope, My Mini Mart, Adorable Home.
- Common DNA across all of them: one-thumb input, first fun < 5s,
  runs under 2 minutes, visible progress, cute or satisfying feedback.

## The drop-and-merge (Suika) opportunity — TAKEN, shipped as game7

Suika Game: 11M+ downloads by Oct 2024, most-downloaded eShop game in
Japan two years running, sequel (Suika Game Planet) launched US
January 2026 — the genre is still growing. Dozens of indie variants
with their own characters are how the genre normally works; the
mechanic is not protectable IP, the fruit art/name are.

Selling points copied into **Critter Drop** (game7):
1. Near-miss tension — you can SEE the big merge you almost made.
2. Cute faces on the physics objects (our critters ARE the balls).
3. Soft, musical feedback per merge (pentatonic ladder by tier).
4. The jar shows your whole run — progress is a picture, no menus.
5. One-thumb aim-and-release; nothing to learn, everything to master.
6. Chain cascades (merge → merge → merge) for jackpot moments.
7. Endgame spectacle: two nocturnix = MOONBURST (both vanish, +200)
   — our twist on the double-watermelon.

## Rules that stay true regardless of trend

- Copy loops and hooks, never art, names, characters, or audio.
- Every game ships with the honest-by-design ad policy: rewarded =
  optional continue, granted only on completed watch; interstitials
  between runs only, ≥60s apart; site + itch builds ad-free.
- Free by design — these are discovery products for the Wildhaven
  world, not paid SKUs.
