# Scout report — how zero-audience sellers actually get first sales (2026-08-22)

Live web research, not memory. Evidence tags: [platform-doc] = official
docs/blog, [data] = third-party dataset, [anecdote] = individual
postmortem/community thread, [guru] = marketing content, low trust.

## Headline findings

1. **The base rate is brutal.** ~44% of Gumroad products never earn
   anything; median time to $500/mo with no audience is 6–12 months; no
   documented overnight zero-audience wins. The one concrete first-sales
   case ($554 over 8 months) was earned by manually posting in relevant
   Reddit threads. [data + anecdote]
   https://insightraider.com/en/answers/is-gumroad-good-for-selling-ebooks

2. **Kobo is the friendliest big retailer for nobodies.** "Free" is the
   most-searched term on Kobo; permafree is native; the KWL Promotions
   tab lets any author apply for merchandising — authors who never
   submit are invisible to it. [platform-doc]
   https://www.kobo.com/kobo-writing-life/blog/sell-more-on-kobo-with-promotions

3. **For the zh-TW book, the real market is Taiwan platforms.** Readmoo
   (讀墨) is the largest zh-TW EPUB store; its mooPub relays to Google
   Play + Apple Books and gives 20 free review-copy codes per title.
   Kobo has served zh-TW since 2016; approval reported in ~12h.
   [platform-doc + anecdote]
   https://deanlife.blog/how-to-publish-ebook-on-readmoo/

4. **Portals beat itch for game traffic.** Median itch page: 1,582
   lifetime views ever. CrazyGames pays ~60% ad revshare (~€1.20/1,000
   plays in one public breakdown — real but small). Poki placement is
   engagement-meritocratic and its Playtesting tool sends up to 50 real
   players free with recorded sessions. [data + platform-doc]
   https://howtomarketagame.com/2025/05/12/benchmark-itch-io-traffic/
   https://developers.poki.com/guide/monetization

5. **Etsy printables: saturated but alive; Q4 window opens NOW.** First
   sales take weeks–months. What works: all 13 long-tail tags,
   persona/occasion tags, more single-purpose listings, refreshed
   mockups; Aug–Dec is the planner/holiday peak. Pinterest is the
   standard free external channel (~20% of one shop's traffic, organic).
   [community + convergent guidance]
   https://getbetterlisting.com/en/blog/etsy-tag-seo-best-practices
   https://digitalbizplr.com/blogs/learn/pinterest-etsy-traffic-strategy

6. **zh finance YouTube monetizes well but slowly.** YPP: 1,000 subs +
   4,000 watch-hours (or 10M Shorts views/90d); early tier at 500 subs.
   Shorts reach non-subscribers by design — the only surface where zero
   subs doesn't matter. Honest timeline: 6–12 months. [platform-doc]
   https://www.studiobinder.com/blog/youtube-monetization-requirements/

7. **New in 2025–26: AI-assistant referrals over-index on tiny sites.**
   LLM referral traffic is small but converts far better than Google
   organic (vendor figures — direction credible, magnitudes soft), and
   the AI share of visits is proportionally highest for sites under
   1,000 visits/mo — exactly us. Being the clear, honest, citable answer
   to one specific question is now a distribution channel. [data-vendor]
   https://indexly.ai/blog/state-of-llm-referral-traffic/

8. **Convergent lesson:** winners post where buyers already SEARCH
   (Etsy search, Kobo free page, Pinterest, portals, niche subreddits) —
   not on follower-based social with zero followers.

## Readmoo addendum to mandarin-book-publish.txt

Store 4 — READMOO 讀墨 (largest zh-TW ebook platform):
1. moopub.readmoo.com → 註冊出版者帳號 (needs owner's bank details)
2. Upload the same EPUB + cover; metadata identical to Kobo listing.
3. Tick relay to Google Play Books / Apple Books if offered (one upload,
   three stores).
4. Use the 20 免費公關書序號 (review-copy codes): send to zh-language
   book reviewers / podcast hosts / IG book accounts with a one-line
   honest pitch. This is the built-in outreach tool — free.

## Explicitly NOT doing

Paid ads (rules), mass thin listings/articles (deindex risk), new
products (catalog is sufficient; distribution is the whole problem),
follower-based social broadcasting with zero followers (documented
viewing-not-buying audiences).
