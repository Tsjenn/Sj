# Scout report — 2026-08-15 (coordinator-run)

Researched: distribution channels with built-in traffic for assets we
already own. Sources checked via web search this session.

## Finding 1 — Web game portals accept direct submissions (BIG)

CrazyGames runs an open developer portal: upload an HTML5 zip with
index.html at the root (exactly the format our packaging scripts
already produce), QA reviews in 1-2 days, and "Basic Launch" needs
ZERO SDK work — they measure player response first, then invite a
Full Launch with their SDK and ad revenue share (their public jam
terms cite developers keeping ~60% of ad revenue; payouts monthly
via Tipalti above €100). Poki is invitation/curated — apply and wait.

Why this matters for us: portals bring their OWN players by the
million. This is the traffic channel that does not depend on the
owner posting anywhere. Five games qualify.

Catch that needs the owner's decision: portal games are free-to-play
and generally cannot carry external store links — so a portal build
earns AD SHARE instead of sales, alongside (not replacing) the paid
itch version. Pricing/business model is the human's call per fleet
rules.
