# Scout report — asset marketplaces & creator channels, verified (2026-08-22)

Fourth research pass of the day: which free channels can turn EXISTING
assets (clipart, music, games, books) into income. Open/closed status
verified against official pages. Tags: [platform-doc] official,
[anecdote] practitioner.

## Open doors (ranked)

1. **Creative Fabrica — OPEN.** Store signup live, non-exclusive, up
   to 75% on referred sales / 50% CF-referred; subscription pool pays
   per download. Realistic: cents per download — income comes from
   LISTING VOLUME, so re-slice the 13-critter pack legitimately: full
   pack + per-animal listings + mini-bundles. Disclose AI assistance
   honestly where applicable (their rule and ours).
   https://www.creativefabrica.com/open-store/

2. **Pond5 — OPEN, non-exclusive by default.** ~30-35% royalty on
   music. ⚠ NEVER join their exclusive program (60% rate) — it would
   kill own-store sales and game-soundtrack bundling. 20+ tracks is a
   viable starter catalog; metadata quality is the main lever.
   https://contributor.pond5.com/faq/

3. **CoolMathGames — OPEN, pays a flat license fee** (historically
   ~$500-2k per accepted game [anecdote — promotional source,
   discount]) for ad-free logic/strategy HTML5 games, NON-exclusive.
   Best $/effort if accepted. Candidate: Wildhaven Arena (strategy).
   https://www.coolmathgames.com/submit-a-game

4. **GameDistribution / Famobi — OPEN aggregators**, non-exclusive ad
   rev-share to thousands of portals. After CrazyGames verdict.
   https://gamedistribution.com/developers/partnership/

5. **TheHungryJPEG — OPEN**, 50% commission, ~10-day review. Duplicate
   the Creative Fabrica listings there; marginal effort.

## Verified walls (don't waste time)

- **AudioJungle**: closed to new authors since 2021, still closed.
- **Envato Elements**: clipart/graphics intake closed.
- **YouTube Audio Library**: no public submission path; articles
  claiming one are junk.
- **Epic!**: publishers with 50+ titles only.
- **Creative Market**: application-gated; thin portfolio = rejection.
- **小紅書 monetization**: needs China bank card — Malaysian owner
  cannot cash out. Discovery mirror only, with mandatory AI-content
  self-labeling (rule since 2026-07). Threads pays nothing directly
  (Meta bonuses dead since ~2025-04) — it is the FUNNEL, monetization
  stays on our store. This confirms the beyond.md top-pick framing.
- **StoryWeaver**: open BUT everything becomes free CC content
  forever — would convert a paid book into a donated one. Only with
  the owner's explicit, informed consent; never by default.
- **Artlist/Uppbeat**: curated, "human-made" positioning; exclusivity
  clauses lurk in Artlist-family contracts — read before signing.

## What agents prep vs what the owner does

Agents can produce ~90%: portal builds, listing images, per-track
metadata, bilingual copy, pitches. The owner does account creation,
payout details, and the final submit click — every channel above
requires that by design.
