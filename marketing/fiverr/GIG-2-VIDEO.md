## Description (1,150 characters)

A 10–30 second vertical video of your book or product turning in 3D
under studio light, with your title, one line of copy, and a call to
action — sized for TikTok, Reels and Shorts, or landscape for YouTube
and Amazon.

You send: the cover or product image (front, and back if you have it),
the one line you want on screen, and where it will be posted.

You get:
• A 3D render of your cover or product, lit and animated in real time
• Your title and one-line hook, typeset to match the cover
• An end card with your call to action (link, store, price — your words)
• Music from a generated, royalty-free score, or silent for your own
• 1080 × 1920 vertical, plus 1920 × 1080 landscape in Standard and above

What you can see before ordering: the clips in my portfolio were made
this way for four published books. The pipeline renders the cover as a
real 3D object, so the light, reflections and edges move like a
physical book, not a flat image.

Built with a Claude Code rendering pipeline (three.js + ffmpeg) and
checked frame by frame before delivery. Every video is made from your
files; nothing is reused between buyers.

Not offered: voice-over, live-action footage, or characters. Ask first
for anything unusual.

## FAQ

**Is the video AI-generated?**
The 3D animation is rendered by a pipeline built with Claude Code; the
render itself is a real 3D scene of your cover, not a generated
hallucination of it. Your artwork is never altered.

**Can I use it on Amazon or in ads?**
Yes. You own the delivered files. The music is generated for your
video and is royalty-free.

**Do you write the copy?**
You supply the line. If you want alternatives, ask in the order and I
will offer three; you choose.

**Will the text be inside the safe zone on TikTok?**
Yes — the end card and hook sit above the area TikTok's interface
covers.
