# Thirty minutes on the iPad: putting the four gigs live

Every field below is copied from the gig files in this folder. Nothing
to write. Do it in Safari on the iPad, not the Fiverr app — the app hides
some seller screens.

## 0. Before you start (5 min)
- Have these open in tabs: GIG-1-WEB-APP.md, GIG-2-VIDEO.md, GIG-3-BOOK.md,
  GIG-4-EXCEL.md, PROFILE.md. Save the four gig PNGs to Photos.
- Save dist/clips/book3d-ledger-live.mp4 to Photos (the gig video).

## 1. Account (5 min)
1. fiverr.com → Join → use the Gmail address. Verify the email.
2. Top right → "Become a seller". Fiverr asks for: full name, display
   name, profile photo, description, languages, skills. Paste from
   PROFILE.md. Photo: a plain headshot, not a logo.
3. Phone verification: your Malaysian number works.
4. Skip "link your accounts". Do not put the employer anywhere.

## 2. Gig 1 (5 min) — repeat for 2, 3, 4
Seller dashboard → Gigs → Create a new Gig.
- **Overview**: Title (paste, it already starts with "I will"), Category
  (as named in the gig file), Search tags (paste the five).
- **Pricing**: three packages. Name, description (paste the "Scope"
  cell), delivery days, revisions, price. Toggle "Extras" and add the
  ones listed.
- **Description & FAQ**: paste the description, then add the five FAQs
  one by one.
- **Requirements**: add the five questions, each as "free text",
  mark all as required.
- **Gallery**: upload the gig PNG. For Gig 2 also upload the video.
- **Publish**.

Fiverr may hold a new gig in "pending approval" for a day. That is normal.

## 3. Payout (5 min)
Settings → Billing and payments → Withdraw → PayPal or Payoneer. PayPal
Malaysia works for receiving from Fiverr. Set it up now, not at the
first order.

## 4. Then, every day (5 min)
- Fiverr app on the iPad with notifications on. Reply to any message
  within the hour.
- When an order arrives, paste the buyer's requirement answers here.
  I build and test; you check and deliver.

## What you are allowed to skip
Fiverr's "Seller Plus" upsell, the "Fiverr Pro" application, and every
prompt to "promote your gig" for money. None of those are needed for a
first order.
