## Description (1,190 characters)

Send a manuscript. Get back the three files Amazon actually accepts:
a reflowable EPUB for Kindle, a print-ready paperback PDF with correct
margins and gutter for your trim size, and a cover that meets KDP's
specification — with a cover for the ebook and a full wrap (front,
spine, back) for the paperback sized to your exact page count.

What is included:
• Clean chapter structure, table of contents, front and back matter
• Typography set for reading: running heads, page numbers, widows and
  orphans controlled
• EPUB validated so KDP's checker passes first time
• Paperback PDF at your trim size (6×9, 5.5×8.5, 5×8, or tell me)
• Cover: typeset title and author, with your artwork or an original
  design brief you approve first
• The KDP upload checklist: categories, keywords, description field
  formatting, and the AI-content disclosure answered correctly

What you can see before ordering: four published titles in my portfolio,
including a 795-page paperback interior and a novel — all built with
this pipeline and live on Amazon.

Built with a Claude Code publishing pipeline and checked in Kindle
Previewer and a print proof before delivery. Nothing is templated: your
book's structure decides the layout.

Not offered: ghost-writing, editing your text, or illustrations. Ask
first about picture books and fixed-layout formats.

## Buyer requirements

1. Attach the manuscript (.docx preferred; Google Docs export is fine).
2. Title, subtitle, author name exactly as they should appear.
3. Trim size for the paperback (or "you choose for a novel / for
   non-fiction").
4. Do you have cover artwork? Attach it, or describe the mood and
   three covers you like.
5. Anything unusual: footnotes, images, tables, poetry, two languages?

## FAQ

**Is any of this AI-generated?**
The layout pipeline is built with Claude Code. Your text is never
changed, and the cover uses your artwork or an original design you
approve. If you want a fully hand-set book by a human typesetter, this
is not that gig.

**Will KDP accept the files?**
The EPUB is validated and the PDF is built to KDP's margin and bleed
rules for your trim size. If KDP rejects a file for a reason inside my
control, I fix it free.

**Do you upload to Amazon for me?**
No — the account must be yours. You get a step-by-step checklist and I
answer questions during the upload.

**How should I answer KDP's AI-content question?**
Honestly, and the checklist tells you how for your specific book.
