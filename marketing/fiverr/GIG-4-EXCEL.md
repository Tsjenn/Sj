## Description (1,160 characters)

A spreadsheet that works: assumptions in their own cells, every month
driven by formulas, scenarios side by side, and a summary page your
whole team can read. Built by a qualified accountant and checked for
formula errors before it leaves.

What I build:
• 12–36 month cash-flow and P&L forecasts, with best / base / worst
• Budget vs actual trackers that update when you paste new data
• Monthly close packs: P&L, balance sheet, bank rec, AR/AP ageing
• KPI dashboards and pricing or break-even calculators
• Clean-up of messy exports into one usable table

How it works:
1. You answer five questions and attach any files you have.
2. I send a working first version by the delivery date.
3. You test it; revisions are included until it does what you need.
4. You receive the Excel or Google Sheets file. You own it.

What you can see before ordering: a sample monthly close pack built
from example data is in my portfolio — open the Dashboard tab.

No pasted values, no hidden hardcodes, no macros you cannot inspect.
Every number traces to an assumption or a source.

Not offered: tax filings, audit, or statutory accounts.

## FAQ

**Are you an accountant?** I am a qualified accountant. This gig builds
spreadsheets; it does not provide tax, audit or statutory accounting
services.

**Will the formulas break when I add data?** No. Inputs are separate
from calculations, and I test by changing every input before delivery.

**Can you fix my existing model?** Yes — send it with Basic and describe
what is wrong.

**Do you use AI?** I use Claude Code to speed up building and to test
for formula errors. Every model is built for your brief and checked by
me.
