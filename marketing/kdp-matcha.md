## Description (paste into the description box)

One green powder. Eight hundred years of history. Sixty-three recipes.

Matcha is the only tea you drink whole — the entire leaf, stone-ground
and whisked into water. That single fact explains its impossible color,
its price, its calm-focus reputation, and its takeover of the world's
cafes. This book follows the whole story, honestly:

- FROM SONG-DYNASTY CHINA TO YOUR CUP — how whisked tea was born,
  crossed to Japan in a monk's luggage, became the tea ceremony, and
  then conquered the modern world as a latte.
- FROM SHADE TO STONE — how real matcha is actually grown, shaded,
  harvested, and ground at thirty grams an hour, and what "ceremonial
  grade" really means (a truth that will save you money).
- THE QUIET CHEMISTRY — caffeine, L-theanine, and antioxidants
  explained without hype, plus a genuinely fair answer to "is matcha
  better than coffee?"
- 63 RECIPES — the perfect traditional bowl, hot and iced lattes,
  sparkling coolers, smoothies, evening low-caffeine drinks, and a
  full baking chapter: cookies, chiffon, roll cake, tiramisu, no-churn
  ice cream, and more. Every recipe with metric and cup measures and
  an honest "the tricky part" note.
- ORIGINAL ILLUSTRATIONS in every chapter.

No miracle claims. No detox promises. Just the leaf, the craft, the
science, and the kitchen — told straight, by someone who thinks the
truth about matcha is more interesting than the marketing.
