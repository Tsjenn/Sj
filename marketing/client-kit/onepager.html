AI for Finance Teams

A practical half-day session, run by a chartered accountant

Your team is already using AI. There is probably no written rule about it, no agreed standard for checking the output, and no record of where it has been used. This session fixes all three in half a day.

Who it is for

Finance and accounting teams of five to fifty. No technical background assumed. It does assume the people in the room are busy and that being wrong has consequences for them personally.

What the session covers

1 · What it actually isA working mental model, and why these tools are most persuasive on the things they have invented.
2 · Writing a usable promptThe six-part frame, the common failures, and prompts for your team's real workflows.
3 · VerificationA checking habit light enough that busy people keep it. Figures, sources, reasoning.
4 · Where not to use itThe situations where the honest professional answer is to close the laptop.
5 · Your policyWe draft your one-page AI use policy in the room, together. You leave with it written.
6 · What to do firstWhich two tasks to start with, scored against a method rather than chosen by enthusiasm.

What you leave with

  • A one-page AI use policy for your team, drafted in the session and ready to circulate
  • A library of reusable prompts adapted to your own workflows
  • A one-page verification card for the team to work to
  • A scoring sheet for deciding what to automate first
  • Written follow-up answers to anything we could not settle on the day

Why me

I am a qualified chartered accountant and the author of two books on this subject — a professional guide to using AI at work, and a financial history of the AI and semiconductor industry read from company filings. I do not sell software, I am not a reseller, and I have no relationship with any AI company. That is why the session can tell you when the answer is not to use it at all.

Format and fee

Half day, on site or online, up to 20 people. Fee on request — it depends on team size, travel and whether you want the follow-up review. A full-day version includes a workshop on your own live tasks.
