**Title:** No phone app can measure your deep sleep — I build one, here's what they actually do

**Body:**
> I build a phone sleep tracker, which is exactly why I want to set the record straight, because the marketing in this category annoys me.
>
> What a phone can genuinely detect: when you fell asleep, total sleep time, how broken your night was, and sleep efficiency. All from movement + sound, and all fairly reliable.
>
> What it can only estimate: the light/deep/REM breakdown. Real staging needs EEG — electrodes on your scalp. Every consumer tracker (wearables included) is inferring stages from movement patterns, and validation studies consistently show stage-level accuracy well below asleep/awake accuracy. If your deep-sleep number swings wildly night to night while you feel the same — that's the measurement, not your brain.
>
> The one thing phone tech is genuinely great at: smart alarms. Light sleep is exactly what movement sensors detect best, so "wake me when I'm already surfacing" actually works.
>
> Happy to answer questions about how the sensing works. (If anyone wants to see the honest version of one of these apps, I made mine free to try in the browser — link in my profile / on request, per sub rules.)
