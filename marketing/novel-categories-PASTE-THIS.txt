FINDING THE CATEGORIES FOR THE AMAH'S DAUGHTER

WHY YOU COULD NOT FIND THEM

The paths I gave you first were Amazon STOREFRONT names - the way
categories look when a shopper browses the shop. KDP's upload picker
uses different wording. That was my mistake, not yours.

In the KDP picker the tree starts at "Fiction", NOT at
"Literature & Fiction". So searching for "Literature & Fiction" finds
nothing.


CHECK THIS FIRST

Near the top of the categories step KDP asks whether the book is
fiction or non-fiction. If that is set to Non-fiction, no historical
fiction category will appear anywhere in the list, no matter what you
search for.

Set it to FICTION. Then the historical categories appear.


HOW TO USE THE PICKER

1. Click "Choose categories" (or Edit categories).
2. There is a search box at the top of the pop-up. Use it. Do not try
   to scroll the whole tree - it is very long.
3. Type: historical
4. Tick up to THREE. You cannot pick more than three at upload.
5. Click Save.


WHAT TO TICK - in order of preference

  1.  Fiction > Historical > World War II
      The occupation years are the heart of the book, and this is one
      of the most actively browsed fiction categories on Amazon.

  2.  Fiction > Sagas
      Search the box for: sagas
      A smaller category, which is the point. Fewer competitors means
      a handful of sales can put you on its bestseller list.

  3.  Fiction > World Literature > Asia
      Search the box for: world literature
      If you do not see an Asia entry, use one of these instead:
          Fiction > Family Life
          Fiction > Historical > General

If "Historical > World War II" does not appear on your marketplace,
take "Fiction > Historical > General" as the first pick instead.


ONE THING TO AVOID

Do not spend all three picks inside Historical. Three historical
categories compete with each other. One historical + one saga + one
world-literature or family-life reaches three different sets of
readers.


AFTER YOU PUBLISH

Amazon also assigns extra browse categories on its own, based on your
keywords and description. Those appear over the following days and you
do not control them directly.

If you later want to be listed in more categories than three, you can
email kdp-support through the KDP Help page and ask. That is a normal
request and they usually action it.


NOTE

Category names and the exact tree differ slightly between marketplaces
(Amazon.com, Amazon.co.uk and so on) and Amazon changes them from time
to time. The search box is the reliable route. Pick the closest match
to the names above from whatever your screen actually offers - the
choice does not have to be word-for-word identical to be right.
