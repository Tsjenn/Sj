CATEGORIES FOR THE AMAH'S DAUGHTER
How to work the KDP picker you have on screen

THE PROBLEM

You have "History" selected in the Category drop-down. That is the
NON-FICTION history shelf - books about real events. That is why the
Placement panel is offering you 16th Century, 17th Century, 20th
Century and so on.

Your book is a novel. It does not belong there.


WHAT TO DO

1. In the Category drop-down (the long list on the left), scroll down
   and choose:

        Literature & Fiction

   It sits between "Law" and "Medical eBooks" in your list.

2. More drop-downs will appear to the right as you go deeper. Follow
   this path:

        Literature & Fiction  >  Genre Fiction  >  Historical Fiction

3. The Placement panel on the right will fill with tick boxes. Tick the
   one closest to the book. If you are offered a "20th Century" or
   "World War II" placement under Historical Fiction, take it - the
   occupation years are the heart of the book. Otherwise tick the
   general Historical Fiction placement.

4. You are allowed THREE placements in total. To add the next one,
   click "Reset" or use the breadcrumb at the top to go back up, then
   come down a different branch.

5. For the second and third, take:

        Literature & Fiction  >  Genre Fiction  >  Sagas

        Literature & Fiction  >  World Literature  >  Asian

   If "Asian" is not offered under World Literature, use:

        Literature & Fiction  >  Genre Fiction  >  Family Life

6. Click "Save categories" when three are ticked.


WHY THIS SPREAD

Historical Fiction is where readers of Lisa See and Min Jin Lee browse.
Sagas is a small category - fewer competitors, so a handful of sales
can reach its bestseller list. World Literature > Asian reaches a
different reader again. Three picks inside Historical would only
compete with each other.


A NOTE ON WHAT I CAN AND CANNOT SEE

I can see your current screen but not the levels below it. The exact
sub-levels under Literature & Fiction differ a little between
marketplaces and Amazon changes them from time to time. If a name
above is not on your screen, take the closest match - it does not have
to be word-for-word identical to be right.


WHILE YOU ARE ON THAT PAGE

Your seven keyword boxes were still empty in the screenshot. Fill them:

    historical fiction
    family saga
    World War 2 fiction
    Malaysia
    multigenerational saga
    Asian historical fiction
    secrets and lies novel

One phrase per box. Keywords are what Amazon uses to place your book in
extra browse categories beyond the three you pick here, so do not skip
them.
