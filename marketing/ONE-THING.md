## The answer

Do rank 1 today and rank 2 this week. They feed each other: every spec
ad is a portfolio piece for the video gig, and every Fiverr video order
is a client who found you without a spec.

Everything else waits. The video you sent says you do not have to go to
work if you have done the right things. The right things here are
numbered one to five, and none of them is a new product.

## What this session cannot do

Open an account in your name, verify your phone, or accept a payout.
Every channel that pays passes through one of those. That is the whole
reason the list has a right-hand column.
