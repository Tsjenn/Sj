What is deliberately not in it

  • No statistics about AI adoption or productivity.
  • No case studies about firms that saved a fortune.
  • No promises about your income, your headcount or your job.
  • No vendor named, endorsed, or quietly recommended.

All four are standard in this market and almost none of it is checkable. The moment a pack like this quotes a figure nobody can verify, every other claim in it becomes a guess too. What that costs you: it cannot tell you what results to expect. What it buys you: everything it does tell you, you can check.

Who it is for

Finance managers and controllers, partners in small accounting and audit firms, tax practitioners, and heads of finance who have realised their team is already using these tools and there is no written rule about it.

It assumes no technical background. It does assume you are busy, that you are sceptical, and that being wrong has consequences that land on you personally.
