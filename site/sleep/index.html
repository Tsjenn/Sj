About the estimates

Rested listens for movement and sound to work out when you were awake, restless or settled, the same way other phone sleep trackers do. It is good at telling asleep from awake, and only approximate at splitting light, deep and dream sleep — those are properly measured with brain activity, which a phone cannot see. Use the trends, not any single number.
