How to play

🧭 Explore: WASD / arrow keys to move (touch buttons on mobile). Your park is in the west — wild creatures roam the lands to the east.

🔵 Catch: get close to a wild creature and press SPACE (or 🎯) to throw an orb. Grab glowing orb pickups to restock.

🏠 House them: caught creatures need a habitat matching their nature (meadow, water, ember, sky, night). Build one and they move in automatically.

🪙 Earn: visitors enter through your park gate and pay admission. Creatures on display earn coins over time — even while you're away. Stalls and decorations attract more visitors.

⭐ Goal: grow your park to a 5-star rating and complete your creature collection!
