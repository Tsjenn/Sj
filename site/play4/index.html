How to race

🏎️ Drive: W / ↑ throttle · S / ↓ brake · A D / ← → steer. R respawns you.

💨 Drift & boost — the heart of the game: hold SHIFT (or SPACE) while turning to break traction. The longer you hold the drift, the bigger the charge: BLUE → VIOLET → PINK. Let go to fire the boost. Chaining drifts through every corner is how you win.

⚡ Nitro: fills as you drift and slipstream behind rivals. Press SPACE on a full bar for an overtake burst.

⏱️ The delta: the number under your lap time is how far ahead (cyan) or behind (pink) your personal best you are, right now. Chase the green.

🌍 Race the world: finish a lap and your run becomes a Race Code. Trade codes with anyone — their ghost appears on your track and you race them for real.
