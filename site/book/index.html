The three rules it was written by
i.

Mechanism over miracle

Every recommendation comes with the why attached — what it does in your body, in plain language. A rule you understand survives a holiday. A superstition doesn't.

ii.

Ranked, not listed

Sleep has a handful of big levers and a long tail of things that barely matter. The chapters are ordered by leverage — and the things that barely matter are named as things that barely matter.

iii.

Honest about the edges

Where evidence is thin, the book says thin. Where your problem stops being a habits problem and starts being a medical one, it says doctor — in plain letters, not fine print.
