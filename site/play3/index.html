How to play

🧭 Move: WASD / arrows. Hold SHIFT to sprint. Touch buttons on mobile.

⚔️ Battle: walk up to a wild critter and press SPACE. Attack, use your type's Special, or throw an orb to catch (works best when their HP is low). Grab glowing orbs to restock.

🧬 Types: 🌿 meadow beats 💧 water beats 🔥 ember beats 🌿 meadow. Sky & night are wildcards.

📈 Train: wins earn XP. Your team of three levels up and gets stronger. Deeper zones = higher-level critters.

👑 Win: defeat the three crowned Guardians to become Arena Champion.

🌍 Duel the world: the Duel button turns your team into a Battle Code. Trade codes with anyone, anywhere, and fight their real team.
