About

What this is

A focus timer. You choose a length, you stay with it, and a small dragon is fed by the minutes you kept. If you leave the app while the ember is lit, the session fades. That single rule is the reason timers like this work: the cost of checking your phone becomes visible.

It makes no claims about productivity, attention or anything else. It is a creature that is hungry when you are distracted.

Privacy

Everything stays in this browser on this phone. No account, no server, no analytics inside the app. Export a file if you change phones.
