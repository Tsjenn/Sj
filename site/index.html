Questions, answered

Do these work with Google Sheets or only Excel?

Both. The spreadsheets open in Microsoft Excel, Google Sheets (upload via Google Drive), LibreOffice, and Apple Numbers. The planner is a standard PDF that any device can open and print.

I'm not in the US — will these work for me?

Yes. All templates are currency-neutral (plain numbers, no fixed currency symbol) and the planner is undated, so they work in any country. The planner PDF ships in both A4 and US Letter sizes.

How do I get my files?

Instantly after purchase — a download link is sent to your email and shown on the confirmation page. You keep lifetime access and can re-download any time.

What if I have a problem with my file?

Reply to your purchase receipt email and we'll make it right — a fixed file or your money back.

Can I share the files with friends?

Each purchase is for your own personal or business use. Sharing or reselling the files isn't permitted — but you can print and reuse them for yourself forever.
