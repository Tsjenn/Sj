How to fly

🪢 Swing: hold SPACE (or the big button) to throw a light-rope at the best anchor ahead. Release to let go. Your momentum carries — release on the upswing to fly.

🎯 Steer: A / D or ← → lean mid-air and mid-swing. W reels the rope shorter for speed.

💨 Flow: chaining swings without touching down builds FLOW — at full bar you glow and move faster. The fog below ends your flow (and costs 3 seconds).

🏮 Deliver: fly through every ring to the goal lantern. Gold, silver and bronze times are set from real measured runs.

🌍 Compete: finish a course and your line becomes a Flight Code — trade codes with anyone, race their golden ghost.
