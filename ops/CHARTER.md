# Clarity — operating charter

Written 19 September 2026. The owner asked to run this as a company with
departments rather than a pile of projects. This file is the company.
It is short on purpose: a charter that nobody reads is not a charter.

## What the company does

Sells work that already exists — services on Fiverr and Upwork, spec
ads to local businesses, digital products on Etsy and Gumroad, books on
Amazon — and builds nothing new until something has sold.

## Departments, and who staffs them

| Department | Staffed by | Runs | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Admin** | Routine "COS — daily standup" | Every morning 08:00 MYT | One email to the owner with today's single task, exact steps, and a reply-to-mark-done loop |
| **Sales** | Owner (sending) + this session (everything else) | Daily | Proposals, gig replies, spec ads finished, follow-ups drafted |
| **Marketing** | Routines "Daily SEO guide writer", "Weekly guide editor"; this session for clips and listings | Daily / weekly | Guides, site, social queue, video clips |
| **Operations** | This session | On every order | The deliverable, tested, handed to the owner to deliver |
| **Finance** | Routine "COS — weekly board" + ops/ledger.csv | Monday 07:00 MYT | Cash in, cash out, fees owed, pipeline value; one page |
| **Board** | Routine "COS — weekly board" | Monday 07:00 MYT | What shipped, what was sent, what earned, what is blocked; appended to AGENT_NOTES.md and emailed |

## The one rule of the company

**Nothing new is built while any item in ops/TASKS.md is undone.** The
task list is ordered by speed to first payment. The daily standup sends
the first undone item and nothing else.

## KPIs (honest ones only)

Counted, never estimated:
- Listings live (Fiverr gigs, Etsy listings, Upwork profile)
- Messages sent (proposals, DMs, spec ads delivered)
- Replies, calls, orders
- Cash received (ops/ledger.csv)

No views, no impressions, no "reach". Those are not money.

## How the owner marks a task done

Reply to the standup email with the word **done** (anything else in
the reply is fine). The next standup reads the reply, ticks the task in
ops/TASKS.md, commits it, and sends the next one. Reply **skip** to
push a task to the bottom of the list, with one line saying why.

## Finance records

ops/ledger.csv, one row per movement: date, channel, description, in,
out, currency. The owner adds a row by replying to any standup with a
line beginning **ledger:** e.g. `ledger: 2026-09-25, Fiverr, Gig 2
order, 150, 0, USD`. Admin writes it to the file.

## What the company will not do

Trade, run ads on a book with no reviews, buy reviews, scrape
followers, automate DMs, promise outcomes, or use protected titles.
