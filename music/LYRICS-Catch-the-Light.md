2. Verses are soft/wistful ("ooh" sections), choruses open up ("aah" sections).
3. Any DAW works (GarageBand is free): import the WAV, record voice on a
   second track, nudge timing, done.
4. Hiring out: search "female pop vocalist demo" on Fiverr ($25–60); send them
