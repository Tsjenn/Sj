# January 2 — The Big Hill

On the second morning of the year, Flufftail pressed her nose to her window and saw that the big hill had turned into one long, smooth, white slide.

All day she thought about it. She thought about it at breakfast, over acorn porridge. She thought about it at lunch, mid-hop. By the time the sky went peach and lavender, she was standing at the top of the big hill with her heart going pit-pat-pit.

It looked much bigger from up here.

"You could sit on your tail," said a small voice. It was Pebblit, who had climbed up too, his pebble cap dusted with snow. "It's the fluffiest sled in Wildhaven."

Flufftail looked back at her big fluffy tail. It swished, as if to say, *I'm ready.*

So she tucked it beneath her, sat down, wiggled once for luck, and pushed off.

The world went *whoosh.* Snow sprayed up in two silver wings. The wind whistled a high happy note through her whiskers, and the trees at the bottom came closer and closer and — *floomp.* She landed in a snowdrift so soft it was like being caught by a cloud.

Flufftail lay there and laughed at the sky.

Then she climbed up and did it again. The second slide was faster. The third was sillier — she went down backwards by accident and decided she had meant to. On the fourth, Pebblit rode along in her lap, squeaking the whole way down like a happy little kettle.

The last slide of the evening was the slow one. The snow had been ridden smooth, the sun was gone, and the winter lantern glowed amber at the middle of the park. Flufftail let the hill carry her down at its own pace — no pushing, just gliding, watching the stars come out one at a time like shy friends.

At the bottom, she didn't get up right away. The snowdrift held her like a big soft hand. Her legs were tired in the good way, the way that means the day was used all the way up.

"Same time tomorrow?" yawned Pebblit.

"Same time tomorrow," said Flufftail.

She hopped home slowly, leaving a line of small neat pawprints beside the long smooth ribbon of the slide. Inside her den it was warm and dim, and her nest of leaves and moss was exactly nest-shaped, exactly right.

Flufftail curled up and wrapped her tail around herself — the fluffiest blanket in Wildhaven, too.

Outside, snow began to fall again, filling in the slide, making it new for tomorrow. And Flufftail slid gently into sleep, which is the easiest downhill of all.
