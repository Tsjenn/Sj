# January 4 — The Snow-Globe Bubble

Snow was falling on Wildhaven again, and Bubbletide floated out into it the way she always did, riding inside her bubble like a wish inside a wish.

She expected the snowflakes to slide right off. Instead, something wonderful happened.

The first snowflake landed on the top of her bubble — and stayed. It sat there, six-pointed and perfect, like a tiny lace star. Then came another, and another, until the whole curve of her bubble was dusted with resting snowflakes, and not one of them popped it.

Bubbletide turned a slow circle in the air. From inside, the flakes made patterns against the gray sky, drifting when she drifted, holding still when she held still.

"I'm a snow globe," she breathed. "I'm a whole entire snow globe."

Well. This was far too good to keep to herself.

She floated to the pond first, where Aquaphin poked up through the ice hole and laughed her bright, splashy laugh. She floated to the meadow's edge, where Flufftail called down from a branch that it was the prettiest thing she'd seen all winter — and it had been a winter absolutely full of pretty things.

But a snow globe must travel gently. Bubbletide learned to move the way snow moves: no hurry, no sharp turns, just drifting from friend to friend while the flakes rode along on the outside like tiny quiet passengers.

Glimmerwing met her by the old oak, moth wings dusted white. Wingbeats are wind, so Glimmerwing hovered extra softly, and the two of them floated together under the winter lantern, watching the amber light shine through the snowflake lace.

"How long will they stay?" Glimmerwing whispered.

"As long as they like," said Bubbletide. "It's their bubble too, tonight."

The snow eased as the sky grew darker. One by one the windows of Wildhaven lit up, and one by one the critters went in to their warm suppers. Bubbletide floated home last of all, slow as a lullaby, her bubble still wearing its stars.

At her sheltered hollow by the reeds, she settled down at the water's edge, bubble resting on the soft snow like a pearl on a pillow.

The snowflakes stayed. She could see them above her when she looked up — tiny and patient, holding their perfect shapes in the cold.

Bubbletide yawned a small round yawn that fogged the inside of the bubble for a moment, then cleared.

The world outside was white and still. The world inside was warm and hers. And she fell asleep in the middle of the gentlest snow globe ever made, which nobody ever, ever needed to shake.
