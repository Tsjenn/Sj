# Clarity Templates — your ready-to-launch digital product store

This repository is a **complete online business in a box**: three finished digital
products, a storefront website, free worldwide hosting, and payment processing that
works in almost every country. Total cost to launch: **$0**.

**Honest expectations:** no store earns money on its own. The products and website
are done for you; sales come from the marketing steps in [MARKETING.md](MARKETING.md).
Most sellers see their first sales within weeks of consistent posting, not overnight.

## Launch checklist (about 30 minutes)

### Step 1 — Set up payments (Gumroad, free)

1. Create a free account at [gumroad.com](https://gumroad.com). It accepts credit
   cards and PayPal from buyers worldwide, pays you out to your bank, and handles
   VAT/sales tax automatically. (Alternatives: Payhip or Lemon Squeezy — same idea.)
2. Click **New product** → type: *Digital product* → upload
   `dist/Budget-and-Savings-Tracker.zip` → set price **$9** → publish.
3. Repeat for `dist/Freelancer-Invoice-Kit.zip` (**$12**) and
   `dist/The-Focus-Planner.zip` (**$7**).
4. Create one more product for the **bundle** (**$19**) and attach all three zips.
5. Upload `dist/Critter-Isles-Full.zip` as a fifth product (**$6**).
6. Copy each product's short link (looks like `https://yourname.gumroad.com/l/xxxx`).

### Step 2 — Connect your store

Open `site/config.js` and paste your four Gumroad links over the `SET-ME`
placeholders. You can also change the store name, tagline, and support email there.
Commit the change. That's the only file you ever need to edit.

### Step 3 — Put the store online (GitHub Pages, free)

1. Merge this branch into `main`.
2. In this repo on GitHub: **Settings → Pages → Source: GitHub Actions**.
3. The included workflow (`.github/workflows/deploy.yml`) publishes the `site/`
   folder automatically on every push to `main`.
4. Your store is live at `https://<your-username>.github.io/<repo-name>/`.

Optional: buy a domain (~$10/year) and point it at GitHub Pages
(**Settings → Pages → Custom domain**) — it makes the store look far more trustworthy.

> The workflow deploys *only* the `site/` folder — your paid product files in
> `products/` and `dist/` are never published to the website.

### Step 4 — Get buyers

Follow [MARKETING.md](MARKETING.md). The short version: list the same products on
**Etsy** (where buyers already search for templates), post on **Pinterest** (the #1
free traffic source for printables), and let the store be your home base.

## Customizing the products

The products are generated by `scripts/build_products.py`. To change categories,
colors, pages, or branding:

```bash
pip install openpyxl reportlab
python3 scripts/build_products.py
```

Then re-zip / re-upload to Gumroad. Changing the visible store (names, prices,
links) never requires touching Python — only `site/config.js`.

## FAQ

**Why Gumroad instead of my own checkout?** Selling worldwide means handling cards,
PayPal, EU VAT, refunds, and fraud. Gumroad does all of that for a fee of ~10% per
sale, with no monthly cost — the right trade-off until you have consistent volume.

**Is this really passive?** After setup, delivery, payment, and taxes are fully
automatic. The non-passive part is marketing; expect a few hours per week at the
start, tapering off as Pinterest/Etsy listings compound.

**Can I add more products?** Yes — that's the growth path. Each new template is a
new card in `site/index.html`, a new entry in `site/config.js`, and a new Gumroad
product. More products = more search entry points = more sales.
